Habesha Hub policy

Privacy

Effective September 29, 2026

Information we collect

Browsing the directory does not require an account. Habesha Hub may receive information that you voluntarily send when submitting, correcting, claiming or reporting a listing, including your name, contact information, relationship to a listing and supporting links.

How information is used

Information is used to review submissions, verify directory information, respond to requests, prevent abuse and maintain the community directory. Habesha Hub does not sell personal information.

Public information

Business contact information may be published when it is already public or supplied for publication. Private residential addresses are not published in housing posts. Individual professionals are handled more conservatively and may have limited contact details.

Third-party services

Listings may link to external websites, maps, government resources, social networks and other third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Retention and requests

Submission and moderation records may be retained to document corrections, prevent duplicate listings and resolve disputes. People may request correction or removal of personal information through the site's contact process.

Children

The site is a general community resource and is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

Questions or corrections? Use the Contact link on the Habesha Hub home page.
