## Source quality notes

- HabeshaLink lists are useful leads but several addresses are outdated (salons, 2013 market list). Always confirm with the business's own page, Yelp/Google, or a second source.
- Some aggregator doctor/lawyer directories appear auto-generated. Use licensing boards and practice websites instead.
