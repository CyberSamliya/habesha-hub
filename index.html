Community housing board

Housing posts change quickly. Each post shows when it was posted and last checked, and older posts are marked for recheck or expired automatically.

Submit a housing post
Active

Checked within the last 14 days and not past its end date.

Needs recheck

Last checked 15 to 45 days ago. Confirm with the poster before making plans.

Expired

Older than 45 days or past its end date. Hidden unless you choose to show it.

Safety: Habesha Hub never publishes a private home address. Contact details appear only when the poster asked for them to be public. Never send a deposit before seeing a place and meeting the landlord or roommate.

Founder & Mission

Meet the person behind Habesha Hub

Meet the Founder
Founder

Samra Ayele

Habesha Hub was founded by Samra Ayele, an Ethiopian-American technology and operational excellence professional based in Ohio. Originally from Ethiopia, Samra created Habesha Hub to make it easier for Ethiopian and Habesha communities across the United States to find useful community resources in one place.

Mission

Community information that is easier to find and trust

The goal is simple: make Ethiopian and Habesha community information easier to find, verify, correct and share while being transparent about where information comes from and when it was last checked.

About the project

Built by the community, for the community

Habesha Hub began as a volunteer effort to make useful information easier to find for Ethiopian families in the United States. Its goal is to connect community discovery with reliable starting points for practical resources.

Trust standard

Useful without pretending to be the authority

Verified: confirmed against a current official or reliable public source. Community listed: publicly listed but not yet independently confirmed. Needs verification: details incomplete or conflicting. Closed and duplicate records are kept for auditing but hidden from search.
