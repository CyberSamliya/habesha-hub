Submit, Correct or Report

Use this form to add a new listing, fix something on an existing one, or flag a problem. Everything is reviewed privately before anything changes on Habesha Hub.

If you lost money or feel unsafe because of a housing post, please also report it to your local police and to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov. This form alone does not notify law enforcement.

Your request is received privately and reviewed before anything changes on Habesha Hub. New and corrected listings show a "community listed" or "needs verification" badge until confirmed. We never publish your email address.
