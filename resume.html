How the resume builder works
  1. Answer each step. Required fields are marked with a red star (*); the others are optional.
  2. Press “Next” to continue. If something is missing, a message under the field tells you what to fix.
  3. Watch your resume update in the live preview as you type.
  4. Your draft saves automatically in this browser. Press “Save draft” any time to be sure.
  5. To continue on another device, press “Download draft backup”, then use “Restore a draft backup” on the other device.
  6. On the last step, review your resume, then download it as PDF, Word or plain text.
  7. The writing helpers only clean up and organize what you wrote. They never add experience, skills or results you did not enter.
