Church teaching: Part 1 to 10

The faith of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church from beginning to end, in ten parts for adults and children to learn together. Go in order: read each part, open its lesson materials, talk about the questions, then mark it complete.

These lessons are an introduction for families. For deeper teaching, ask your parish priest or Sunday school (Sembete Timhirt Bet).
