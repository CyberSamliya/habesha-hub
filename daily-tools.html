Your device, your information. Planner entries stay in this browser unless you download or share them. They do not sync between devices. Anyone using this browser profile can access saved entries; avoid sensitive information on shared devices. External resource links open other websites.
How Daily Life Tools work
  1. Choose a tool from the list. Each tool opens with its own step-by-step directions.
  2. Fill in the form and press the button under it to save. Fields marked as required must be filled in first.
  3. Your entries are saved in this browser on this device only. Open the page in the same browser to see them again.
  4. Press "Download my planner data" to save a backup file of every tool, and keep it somewhere private.
  5. On a new phone or computer, press "Restore from a backup" and choose that file to bring your entries back.
  6. Press "Print current tool" to print the list in the tool that is open.
  7. Press "Clear my data" to remove everything from this browser. You will be asked to confirm first.
  8. Press "Switch to አማርኛ" at the top to use the tools in Amharic.
