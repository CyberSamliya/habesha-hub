Habesha Hub policy

Accessibility

Effective September 29, 2026

Our approach

Habesha Hub aims to make community information usable by people with different abilities, devices and assistive technologies. The site uses semantic headings, labeled form controls, keyboard-operable controls, a skip link, status announcements and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

The directory is continually updated and some third-party websites linked from listings may not meet the same accessibility standards. Amharic and English content may also differ while translations are reviewed.

Feedback

If a page, control or listing is difficult to use with a keyboard, screen reader, zoom or another assistive technology, contact Habesha Hub with the page and problem so it can be reviewed.

Questions or corrections? Use the Contact link on the Habesha Hub home page.
