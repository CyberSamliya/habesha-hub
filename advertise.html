Trust comes first

Payment does not buy verification

Featured and Sponsored describe paid visibility. Verified describes the directory's factual review process. A business cannot pay to become Verified, and paid placement is labeled so visitors can distinguish advertising from directory trust signals.

Community access

Searching remains free

People using Habesha Hub do not pay to search the directory or access community-resource links. Standard eligible listings can remain free. Habesha Hub does not sell submitter information.

Digital services

Help beyond a directory listing

Some community businesses need more than visibility. Available work can include a simple web presence and practical digital improvements. These services are separate from directory verification and advertising.
